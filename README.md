# BloxZ

<sub>**BETA V2.0.0 — RELEASE**</sub>

Welcome to **BloxZ**!

BloxZ is a gaming project featuring multiple game modes, multiplayer functionality, and ongoing development by Pixlect Studio.

---

## 🎮 Game Modes

### SPEEDRUNNERS
Race through challenging levels and compete with other players.

### DEMO
Explore experimental gameplay and test features.

### FALLING ICE
Survive the falling ice challenge! Once at least 3 players enter the ice lobby, a 20-second countdown begins, allowing additional players to join before the round starts.

---

## 📱 Mobile Compatibility Notice

BloxZ is primarily designed for desktop screens.

On mobile devices, the screen layout might appear **congested, cramped, or improperly arranged**. Some interface elements may not display as intended.

For the best experience, playing on a desktop or laptop is recommended.
